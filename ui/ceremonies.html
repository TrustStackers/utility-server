How to read a step

→ request is what an actor asked for; ← response is what the stack decided or did about it. A allowed badge means a policy gate said yes, denied means it refused (expand the step to see why), and completed means the work was done.

Steps are shown in record order. A ceremony appears once its first step has been recorded.
