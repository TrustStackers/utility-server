Recovery Instructions

For Agent Users

To back up your VTA identity to this utility server, use the agent-app's publishToInfrastructure() method or the POST /identities/remote endpoint with your DID document and public keys. Your secret keys never leave your device.

For Restoration

To restore from backup, use the agent-app's syncFromInfrastructure() method. This fetches your DID document, rotation log, and public key material from the utility server and bootstrap infrastructure. You will still need your seed phrase or root keypair to sign.
